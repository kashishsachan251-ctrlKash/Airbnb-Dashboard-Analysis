# Airbnb-Dashboard-Analysis
🌍 Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

The Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Airbnb listings across major global cities. The dashboard evaluates listing growth trends, market share distribution, pricing structure, host performance, and customer ratings to uncover strategic business insights.

The analysis covers 279,712 listings, 182,024 hosts, 144 property types, across 10 major cities, with over 5.3M reviews.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze Airbnb’s growth lifecycle (Introduction → Growth → Maturity → Decline → Reinvention → COVID-19 Impact)

Identify top-performing cities by listings and reviews

Compare pricing across property types

Evaluate customer satisfaction metrics

Assess superhost contribution and market concentration

📊 Key Insights
📈 1. Growth & Lifecycle Trends

Airbnb experienced peak listing growth in 2015.

2016–2017 showed moderated expansion due to tightening regulations.

2018 marked renewed growth momentum.

2019–2020 witnessed a sharp decline driven by the COVID-19 pandemic.

This reflects Airbnb’s transition from rapid expansion to market maturity and external shock adaptation.

🌎 2. Market Concentration by City

Paris, New York, and Sydney account for nearly 50% of total listings and 48% of total reviews, indicating strong geographic concentration.

Paris leads in both listings and reviews, making it the dominant Airbnb market among analyzed cities.

This suggests that a few global cities drive a significant portion of Airbnb’s overall platform performance.

💰 3. Pricing Analysis (Average Price by Property Type)

Hotel Room – $800

Entire Place – $673

Shared Room – $580

Private Room – $462

Hotel rooms are priced significantly higher, potentially influencing demand distribution across property types.

⭐ 4. Ratings & Customer Satisfaction

Overall ratings across cities remain consistently high (above 9.0).

Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro rank among the highest-rated cities.

Hong Kong and Istanbul show relatively lower overall ratings.

Cleanliness and value-for-money are generally the lowest scoring dimensions.

This highlights that operational quality (cleanliness & value perception) remains a critical improvement area.

🏆 5. Host Performance

Superhosts contribute significantly to listings in major cities.

Higher-rated cities often correlate with stronger Superhost presence.

This reinforces the importance of host quality in driving customer satisfaction and repeat demand.

📌 Business Implications

Heavy reliance on top cities exposes Airbnb to regional risk.

Pricing gaps between property types may impact customer segmentation strategy.

Maintaining high service quality is crucial for sustaining strong ratings.

Regulatory changes and global events significantly influence platform growth dynamics.

🚀 Conclusion

This dashboard provides a strategic view of Airbnb’s global performance by combining growth trends, market share analysis, pricing strategy, and customer satisfaction metrics. The insights support data-driven decision-making for market expansion, pricing optimization, and quality improvement initiatives.
